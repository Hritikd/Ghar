The vision

A family assistant, built for India.

Start with school circulars and household deadlines. Earn a place in the family’s daily routine.

Why AI belongs here

Messages arrive in different formats and languages. The product should understand the context, extract the obligation and make ownership clear.

What I would test

Do families return weekly? Do both partners participate? Are fewer tasks missed? Would households pay ₹99/month? These are hypotheses, not traction claims.
